ProofGrid — voice-over guide

Used-car listings make reassuring claims. But a claim is not proof.

ProofGrid separates what the seller says from what can actually be verified.

Paste a listing, and ProofGrid extracts the important evidence-bearing claims — without inventing a vague trust score.

Each claim is matched with the evidence that would actually support it.

See real documentation? Mark that claim as verified. The Proof Grid updates claim by claim as evidence is checked.

And if something raises a red flag, it becomes a concern — not something hidden inside one score.

Every remaining evidence gap becomes a concrete next action. ProofGrid turns uncertainty into specific questions you can send to the seller.

It is a verification aid — not a vehicle-quality score or purchase recommendation.

Know what is claimed. Know what is proven. Know what to ask next.
